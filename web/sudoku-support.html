Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9, each exactly once. Tap How to Play on the home screen for a quick guide to notes, Quick Pencil, mistakes and hints.

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the Premium page and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On the Premium page, tap Manage or cancel subscription. You can also go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I keep my progress on another device?

Sign in with Apple or Google on the Profile screen. Your stats, coins and streak sync to every device where you sign in with the same account.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, open Settings and tap Delete Account. You can also email us to request deletion. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows ads to support development. Premium removes all ads.
