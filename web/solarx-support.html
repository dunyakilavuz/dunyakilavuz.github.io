Frequently asked questions

The compass points the wrong way

Move your phone in a figure-8 a few times, away from metal and magnets. SolarX shows the same hint when the compass reading is unreliable. Magnetic cases, MagSafe accessories, car mounts, laptops and speakers can all pull the compass off, so remove them or step away. Also check that iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services → Compass Calibration is on.

The Sky view doesn't move when I move my phone

Sky view follows your phone with the motion sensors and compass. Close the App completely and open it again, then hold the phone up and move it slowly. If motion sensors aren't available, you can still drag the sky with your finger to look around.

I denied camera access. How do I turn it back on?

Open iOS Settings → SolarX and turn on Camera. Without the camera, Sky view still works on a black background. SolarX uses the camera only to show the real sky live behind the labels; nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

Why does SolarX need my location?

To calculate where the planets, Moon, Sun and stars are in your sky, and when they rise and set. SolarX asks only for “While Using the App” access, never “Always”, and uses it on your device only. If you don't want to share it, choose Enter manually in Settings → Location and type your coordinates. Without either, SolarX uses Greenwich, UK. To change permission, go to iOS Settings → SolarX → Location.

How accurate is it? Does it need the internet?

Everything is calculated on your device, so it works offline. Eclipse times were checked against NASA's eclipse catalogue for 2001–2100 and land within about a minute of NASA's times. Rise and set times assume a clear, flat horizon, so hills or buildings can make an object appear later or disappear earlier. Only ads, buying or restoring SolarX Pro, and the optional satellite and comet orbit data download from dunyakilavuz.com/solarx-data need a connection. The free version also downloads a small ad settings file from the same site; no personal data is sent.

How do I restore SolarX Pro?

Tap Restore purchases on the SolarX Pro page or in Settings. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On the SolarX Pro page, tap Manage or cancel subscription. You can also go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why do I see ads, and how do I change my ad consent?

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob to support development. SolarX Pro removes all ads: for subscribers the ad SDK is not started at all, so there's no consent form and no ad requests. If you live in a region where consent is required, such as the EEA or the UK, you can review or change your choice in Settings → Privacy & ads → Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere, that option shows that no choice is required.

Does SolarX work on iPad?

Yes. SolarX runs on iPhone and iPad, with a layout made for the larger screen.

Which languages are available?

English and Turkish. SolarX follows your device language by default. To change it, go to Settings → Language in the App.

How do I contact you?

Email dunyakilavuz@gmail.com. Screenshots help a lot when something looks wrong in the sky. The “Spot a typo or translation error? Help us fix it” row in the App only opens a draft email in your mail app; nothing is sent automatically.
